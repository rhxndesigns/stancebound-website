Headlight restoration in Carmarthenshire

Cloudy, hazy or yellowing headlights can make a car look tired. STANCEBOUND offers a headlight restoration service designed to improve the appearance and clarity of suitable lenses.

Request a booking on our main websiteEmail an enquiry

What the service involves

The condition of each lens is assessed before work is agreed. Results depend on the age, condition and type of damage, and restoration cannot fix every defect.

Where we work

Enquiries are welcome from Llanelli, Carmarthen, Ammanford, Swansea and surrounding areas. Contact STANCEBOUND with photos of your headlights to discuss suitability.
