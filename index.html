BEHIND STANCEBOUND

Meet Ryan

Automotive photography and detailing, built from a genuine interest in cars and the culture around them.

FOUNDER · AUTOMOTIVE PHOTOGRAPHER · DETAILER

Ryan Thomas

STANCEBOUND brings together three things I've always been drawn to: cars, creativity and attention to detail. My creative background started with Creative iMedia and Art & Design at college before I went on to study Photography at university. I've always been the sort of person who can spend hours on a project, working on the smallest details until I'm genuinely happy with the result.

Cars have been part of my life for as long as I can remember. I grew up loving heavily modified car culture, from wild body kits and Corsa Lambo doors to the cars my dad was passionate about, including several Subaru Imprezas and a classic Ford RS. After losing my dad, that connection with cars became even more meaningful to me.

Detailing became another creative outlet. I enjoy slowing down, focusing on the tiny details and watching a car gradually come together. I'm BICSc qualified in cleaning, and I'm now developing those skills further within automotive detailing while combining them with my background in photography.

STANCEBOUND is still in its early days, and that's part of what excites me. As it grows, I'll be developing my skills in areas such as headlight restoration, scratch removal, machine polishing and paint correction. The aim is simple: keep learning, improve with every car, and build something I'm genuinely proud to put my name behind.

CUSTOMER AGREEMENT · VERSION 1

STANCEBOUND Customer Agreement

Customer & booking

The person making and accepting the booking must be 18 or over and have authority to authorise work on the vehicle.

Vehicle condition

Please disclose known damage, faults, loose trim, paint defects, electrical issues or modifications that may affect the work. Existing defects may be photographed before work begins.

Access & working area

You must provide safe, reasonable access to the vehicle and an appropriate working area. STANCEBOUND does not require access to your home unless separately agreed and necessary for the service.

Children & pets

Children and pets must be kept clear of the working area while work is taking place because electrical equipment, cables, pressure washing, chemicals and machine-polishing equipment may be in use. The customer is responsible for helping keep the area clear and safe.

Results & limitations

STANCEBOUND will use reasonable care but cannot guarantee that every stain, scratch, mark, odour or existing defect can be removed or corrected.

Personal belongings

Please remove valuables and personal belongings from areas being worked on. STANCEBOUND will take reasonable care while carrying out the agreed service.

Price & additional work

Any additional work or price changes will be discussed with you before that extra work is carried out.

Cancellation & access

Weather, unsuitable access, vehicle condition or other circumstances may mean work needs to stop or be rearranged. Any communicated cancellation or deposit terms also apply.

Photography & media

Promotional photography is only used where you separately agree. STANCEBOUND will not intentionally include personal, address or identifying information in promotional material.
